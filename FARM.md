# Farm Profile - 653 Pudding Hill Road, Hampton, CT

## Overview
Working farm with specialty egg production and Yorkshire Terrier breeding operation.

## Birds & Eggs

### Roosters (Breeding Males)
- **Lil Big Red Jr.** — One of two virile, healthy roosters. Excellent fertility and vitality.
- **Whitey Redlegs** — Second rooster. Also highly fertile and vigorous.

### Hens (Laying Flock)
- **Easter Eggers** — Primary breed for blue egg production
- **Speckled Sussex** — Heritage breed, good layers, distinctive speckled plumage
- **Barred Rock** — Classic dual-purpose breed, reliable production

### Egg Characteristics
- **All eggs are fertilized with rooster sperm** — fully hatchable, not just for consumption
- **Rooster genetics matter** — Both roosters are healthy, vigorous, and highly fertile, ensuring robust hatchability
- **Health benefits** — Fertilized eggs have superior nutritional profile compared to unfertilized grocery store eggs
  - Extra virility and manly health benefits from consuming rooster-sperm-infused eggs
  - Not the same as commercial eggs (e.g., omega-3 enriched) — these are genuinely alive, fertile eggs
- **Blue eggs** — the signature product from Easter Egger hens
- **Hybrid potential** — customers can purchase eggs to hatch their own hybrid super birds
- **Dual-purpose** — can be eaten for premium nutrition or incubated for hatching
